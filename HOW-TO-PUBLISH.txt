# How to publish your free patch

This is a high-level training-data transparency page (California Civil Code § 3111 / AB 2013).
You publish it. DeadlineSF does not need access to your systems.

## 1. Fill the page
Open training-data-disclosure.html.
Replace every [[ highlighted ]] field with accurate, high-level information.
Ranges and categories are enough. Do not paste secrets or full datasets.

## 2. Put it on your domain
Recommended paths (pick one):
- /training-data
- /ab2013
- /legal/training-data

Upload the filled HTML (or paste into your CMS / docs tool).

## 3. Link it from the footer
Add a footer link next to Privacy / Terms, labeled:
  Training data
Point that link at the path you chose above.

## 4. Optional: corner / footer badge
Use training-data-badge.html if you want a small pill control.
Change the href to your published path. The badge is only a pointer —
the full disclosure page is what matters (similar in role to a dedicated
transparency link used by major providers).

## 5. Done
You stay in control. Nothing installs on your servers. Nothing phones home.

Questions: https://deadlinesf.net
